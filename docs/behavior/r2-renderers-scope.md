# The dynamic-lighting renderers (scoping specification)

Scoping specification, for S.T.A.L.K.E.R.: Shadow of Chernobyl (Russian edition), of the game's second
renderer: the two entries of the options' renderer list `renderer_r2a` ("Динамическое освещение объектов")
and `renderer_r2` ("Полное динамическое освещение"), both of which load the second renderer
(`level-loading.md` section 6). Section 1 lists what makes up the second renderer and how the two entries
differ; section 2 the data it reads that the first renderer does not; section 3 every `r2_*` console
variable; section 4 proposes a split into small specs, one format or one behaviour each, with sizes and an
order; section 5 is the completeness pass. It states no behaviour in detail. What could not be established
is marked **unestablished**.

Builds on (not repeated): `docs/behavior/level-loading.md` (section 6, renderer choice),
`docs/behavior/options-screens.md` (the renderer list, the controls of the video screens, the variable
kinds and the presets), `docs/formats/level.md`, `docs/formats/level-geom.md` (the role of `level.geomx`),
`docs/formats/ogf.md` (chunk 22), `docs/behavior/static-shading.md` (`shaders.xr`, scripted definitions,
texture resolution), `docs/behavior/dynamic-lights.md` (the light record, `level` chunk 6),
`docs/behavior/light-shadows-projection.md`, `docs/behavior/glows.md`,
`docs/behavior/hanging-lamps.md` (hanging-lamp light flags), `docs/behavior/torch.md`,
`docs/behavior/sky.md`, `docs/behavior/particle-drawing.md`, `docs/formats/ltx-config.md`.

Conventions: "the first renderer" is `bin\xrRender_R1.dll`, "the second renderer" `bin\xrRender_R2.dll`.
"Programs" are the HLSL source files (`.vs`, `.ps`) under `shaders\r2\`; "scripted definitions" its `.s`
files. Counts of archive files are of the merged archive view (a later archive wins). Every fact carries
the name of the check that reproduces it, a script in `checks/r2-renderers-scope/` of the dirty workspace
(`run_all.py` runs them all; it needs `checks/particles-and-lights-scope/extract_all.py` run first).
Facts marked "static analysis" were established by reading the binaries' code; the checks reproduce them
from the decompiler output kept in `checks/r2-renderers-scope/out/` (regenerated by `ghidra_run.py`).

## 1. What makes up the second renderer

### 1.1 Program files

| File | Size | CRC-32 |
|---|---|---|
| `bin\xrRender_R1.dll` (first renderer, for comparison) | 438,272 | 0x9836F62F |
| `bin\xrRender_R2.dll` (second renderer) | 540,672 | 0xF000B305 |

(`binary.py` B1.) The decompiler finds 1,384 functions in the second renderer and 1,289 in the first
(`binary.py` B1); much of the second renderer's code handles the same data as the first (models, particles,
detail objects, the shader library). Besides the second renderer itself, the second renderer's behaviour
involves `bin\XR_3DA.exe` (renderer choice, weather values, texture descriptions) and `bin\xrGame.dll`
(game objects' lights and the hardware test, 1.4).

### 1.2 `renderer_r2a` and `renderer_r2`

- Both values load the second renderer (`level-loading.md` section 6). The executable gives the second
  renderer one value derived from the renderer variable: on for `renderer_r2a` (list index 1), off for
  `renderer_r2` (index 2) (static analysis; `binary.py` B5).
- The second renderer reads that value in one place: when it is on, it defines the macro
  `USE_R2_STATIC_SUN` for every program it compiles (`binary.py` B5). No other use of the value was found
  (static analysis). 21 files of `shaders\r2\` (programs and include files) test the macro
  (`shaders.py`); by their text (`shaders.py`), with it the sun's light is taken from sun-occlusion data stored with the
  static geometry (light-map alpha or vertex data) and added in the combining pass (`combine_1.ps`),
  instead of by the sun's own pass. **Disputed in review:** "instead of by the sun's own pass" is unbacked: the sun-pass programs (`accum_sun`, `accum_sun_near`, `accum_sun_far`) also test the macro and still compute the sun's light under it, with a fixed material value; since the value's only use found is the macro, whether the sun's own pass runs with `renderer_r2a` is unestablished. What that means in full is spec R11.
- The options screens enable the sun check boxes (`r2_sun`, `r2_sun_details`) only with `renderer_r2`
  (options-screens.md section 6.3).

### 1.3 Command-line switches

The second renderer looks for 17 switches the first does not: `-nodf24`, `-r4xx`, `-nonvs`, `-smap1536`,
`-smap2048`, `-smap2560`, `-smap3072`, `-smap4096`, `-gloss ` (followed by a number), `-bug`, `-sunfilter`,
`-sjitter`, `-depth16`, `-noshadows`, `-tsh`, `-mblur`, `-no_occq`; both renderers look for `-nodistort`,
`-disasm`, `-skinw`, `-ss_tga` (`binary.py` B3). Without a `-smap…` switch the shadow-map size is the one
`-smap2048` selects (static analysis; `binary.py` B9).

### 1.4 Hardware and the hardware test

The second renderer compiles its programs at run time from the sources in `shaders\r2\`, defining up to 19
macros from the hardware's capabilities, the switches above and its own state: `SMAP_size`, `FP16_FILTER`,
`FP16_BLEND`, `USE_HWSMAP`, `USE_HWSMAP_PCF`, `USE_FETCH4`, `USE_SJITTER`, `USE_BRANCHING`, `USE_VTF`,
`USE_TSHADOWS`, `USE_MBLUR`, `USE_SUNFILTER`, `USE_R2_STATIC_SUN`, `FORCE_GLOSS`, `SKIN_COLOR`,
`SKIN_NONE`, `SKIN_0`, `SKIN_1`, `SKIN_2` (`shaders.py`). It names 27 render targets and generated
textures with the prefix `$user$` (among them `$user$position`, `$user$normal`, `$user$albedo`,
`$user$accum`, `$user$material`, `$user$smap_depth`, `$user$bloom1`, `$user$luminance`, `$user$tonemap`,
`$user$sky0`, `$user$sky1`, `$user$env_s0`, `$user$env_s1`); the first renderer names 5 (`binary.py` B8). **Disputed in review:** one of the 27 strings, `$user$jitter_`, is a stem the renderer completes with a digit to form `$user$jitter_0` – `$user$jitter_3` (also among the 27); the distinct names are 26.
It builds a volume (three-dimensional) texture, and only it names `$user$material` and the program
constant `L_material` (`binary.py` B13). The hardware test that decides whether the renderer list is offered is
started from `bin\xrGame.dll` (`binary.py` B11); what it checks is **unestablished** (options-screens.md
section 17).

The second renderer's binary names 43 of the program names directly, among them every `bloom_` and
`shadow_direct_` name and 13 `accum_` names (`shaders.py`); the others are reached through definitions.
32 scripted definitions have a namesake in `shaders\r1\`; only `shaders\r2\` has `distort.s` and
`models_metallreflect.s`; only `shaders\r1\` has `blur2.s`, `blur4.s`, `postprocess_d.s` (`shaders.py`).
Four scripted definitions of `shaders\r2\` (`models_xglass2`, `models_xwindows`, `models_xlens`,
`models_xmonolith`) name the texture `$user$projector`, which the second renderer's binary does not name
(`shaders.py`); what they then sample is **unestablished**.

tags (`BmmDold ` 69, `S_SET   ` 51, `MODEL   ` 23, `MODELEbB` 11, `LmEbB   ` 5, `V_AREF  ` 5,
`PARTICLE` 5, `D_TREE  ` 3, `V       ` 3, `LM_AREF ` 2, `D_STILL ` 2, `LM      ` 1, `E_SEL   ` 1,
`E_WIRE  ` 1, `SH_WORLD` 1) (`shaders.py`); the second renderer turns each class into its own passes.

### 4.2 Order

Each after its dependencies: R1, R2, R3, R4, R6, R7, R13, R11 (a lit frame with sun and ambient light,
no shadows, as `renderer_r2a` draws it), R8, R10, R16, R17, R5, R14, R18, R9, R12 (the full dynamic
lighting of `renderer_r2`), R15 last (off by default). R16 comes before the shadow specs because the shipped settings
turn tone mapping on (`r2_tonemap on`; `settings.py`).

### 4.3 Size

The whole area is larger than the first renderer's specs together: 161 programs and 5,300 lines of
program sources against `shaders\r1\`'s 146 files (1.5), 52 variables, 17 switches. R3, R4, R6, R8, R9,

choice of casters and the map passes; R12 into the near/far parts and the perspective variant; R14 into
particles, water and the remaining surfaces; R3 into pass order and visibility.

## Gaps found in review

- Every chunk-6 light receives the mark that limits it to `r2_allow_r1_lights`, which is off built in and in the shipped `user.ltx`; whether any later step clears that mark, and so whether chunk-6 point lights are drawn by default, is not stated (R8).
- What the shared switches `-disasm`, `-skinw` and `-ss_tga` (screenshot format) do in the second renderer is named in no spec's description beyond R1's classification.
- What the executable draws through the second renderer outside the level's surfaces (interface, HUD, lens flares, rain, thunderbolts) is named in no spec's description; `simple`, `simple_color` and `hud_*.s` sit in R3 and R5 without that scope.
- The `accum_sun_near_*` variants are assigned to R12 by section 5 but named in R2's description as possibly never loaded; one spec should own that question.
- The sun's own pass (`accum_sun`) is described under R11 but its files are assigned to R12.
